RealmHunt Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026 · Effective for: RealmHunt (iOS)

Who we are

RealmHunt is an augmented-reality treasure hunt game published by Nathan Liang, an individual developer. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the app, why, and what choices you have.

Questions about this policy or your data: askrealmhunt@rabbitninja.co

Information we collect

Account information. You can create an account with email/password, or sign in with Google, Apple, or Game Center. Signing in with Google or Apple shares your email address (or, if you choose Apple's "Hide My Email" option, a private relay address that forwards to you) and the display name you choose — we never receive your password for any of these. Signing in with Game Center shares your Game Center player identity, which our server verifies directly with Apple; we don't receive your Apple ID or password. You can also play as a guest — an anonymous account with no email or personal information, just a fun display name you pick — whether jumping in solo or joining a one-off group hunt via Party Kit. A guest account can later be linked to a Google, Apple, or email/password identity from Settings, so progress isn't lost if the app is reinstalled or you switch devices.

Precise location. RealmHunt is a location-based game — we collect your device's GPS location while you're playing to show you nearby treasures, place them realistically in AR, and track your progress through a hunt. Location is only collected while the app is in use, not in the background.

Camera. AR mode uses your camera to overlay treasure items on your live surroundings. This is a real-time passthrough only — RealmHunt does not record, store, or transmit your camera feed. Some features let you save or share an AR screenshot; that only happens if you explicitly choose to.

Gameplay data. Progress, level, in-game currency, items, pets, and hunt history, tied to your account so it persists across sessions and devices.

Friends and social features. If you add friends in-app, we store that connection so features like live proximity alerts and item trading work. We never share your precise location with a friend — only relative distance ("Sam is 200m from a treasure you haven't found").

Push notification token. If you allow notifications, we store a device token (from Apple) to send you gameplay alerts (e.g. a live event starting). No content of notifications is shared with anyone outside RealmHunt.

Purchases. In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see or store your payment card details — Apple handles that relationship directly, and we only receive confirmation that a purchase succeeded.

Crash and error reports. If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, we automatically send a basic technical report (what happened, app/OS version, device model) to help us fix bugs. This is not tied to your name — only your account ID, if you were signed in at the time.

Diagnostic device info. App version, OS version, and device model may be included with crash/error reports and support requests to help us reproduce problems.

Children's privacy (Family Mode)

RealmHunt's Family Mode lets a parent link a child's account to their own and organize hunts together. This is the only way a child's location is ever collected in the app, and it requires the parent to actively link the account first (via a 6-digit code shown only in the parent's own app) — this linking step is our verifiable parental consent mechanism.

  • A child's location is only tracked during an active family hunt the parent set up, using the latest position only (not a location history).
  • Parents can see their child's live location during an active hunt and receive a safety alert if the child leaves a set boundary.
  • We do not serve targeted advertising to anyone, and RealmHunt has no analytics or ad-tracking SDK integrated at all — nothing in the app profiles behavior for advertising purposes, for children or adults.
  • Guest mode (whether playing solo or joining a group hunt, e.g. a birthday party, via Party Kit) collects zero personal information from any participant, including children — anonymous sign-in, a chosen display name only.

If you're a parent and have questions about your child's data, or want it removed, contact askrealmhunt@rabbitninja.co — see also Account Deletion below, which a parent can use on their own linked family account.

How we use your information

  • Operating the core game: showing you nearby treasures, tracking progress, syncing your account across devices.
  • Safety features in Family Mode (geofence alerts).
  • Social features you opt into (friends, trading, live proximity pings).
  • Fixing bugs and technical problems (crash/error reports).
  • Fraud/abuse prevention for in-game economy and purchases.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for targeted advertising — RealmHunt has no ad or ad-analytics SDK in the app at all.

Who we share information with

  • Firebase/Google Cloud — our backend hosting, authentication, and database provider. However you sign in (email, Google, Apple, Game Center, or guest), your account and gameplay data is ultimately stored there.
  • Google Gemini API — used to generate the riddles/clues you see in hunts. Approximate location context (e.g. a nearby landmark name, not your precise coordinates or identity) may be sent to generate location-relevant clue text.
  • Apple — if you choose Sign in with Apple or Game Center, push notifications (APNs), and all purchases (StoreKit). Game Center sign-in works by our server verifying your identity directly with Apple's servers before creating your RealmHunt session.

We do not share your data with data brokers or advertisers, and we do not sell it to any third party.

Your choices

  • Location: you can deny or revoke location permission in iOS Settings at any time. Core gameplay requires it, so the app won't be able to show you nearby treasures without it.
  • Notifications: optional, controlled in iOS Settings.
  • Account deletion: you can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account in the app. This removes your profile, gameplay progress, and personal identifiers. Some anonymized historical data that doesn't identify an active account on its own (e.g. aggregate leaderboard/statistics entries) may be retained in a form that no longer references your deleted account.

Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile and personal data are removed immediately; see Account Deletion above for what's retained in anonymized form.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the app and our servers and token-based authentication. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date; continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
